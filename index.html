Common Tile Issues

We help fix the problems homeowners notice most.

Loose Tile

Loose or hollow tiles can come from weak bonding, poor prep, or movement underneath the tile.

Cracked Grout

Cracked grout can make a project look unfinished and may point to movement or installation problems.

Poor Workmanship

Uneven cuts, bad spacing, and crooked layout can ruin the finished look. We focus on clean details.
